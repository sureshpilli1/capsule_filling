What does ZRO mean?
According to ACG, ZRO is based on Zero Relative Motion (ZRM) technology.
Let's break it down:
Relative motion = movement of one object with respect to another.
Zero Relative Motion = at the critical filling point, the powder dosing mechanism and capsule move together at the same speed, so there is almost no relative movement between them.

Why is this important?
In ZRO technology:
Powder enters the capsule more smoothly.
Powder wastage is reduced.
Dust generation is minimized.
Better fill-weight accuracy is achieved.
Difficult powders (sticky, fluffy, hygroscopic, heat-sensitive powders) can be handled more effectively.

What You Should Observe During Changeover
When maintenance assembles the AF90T, watch these items:
	Dosing disc thickness. 
	Tamping pin penetration depth. 
	Scraper plate setting. 
	Powder level in dosing bowl. 
	Transfer pin alignment. 
These five settings control most fill-weight issues on AF90T. 
One thing I'd like to know from your machine: Can you physically see 5 separate tamping pin blocks arranged around the dosing disc, or do you see one assembly containing all 5 pins? Different AF90T installations can make them look very different even though the principle is the same.




























The wiper block is one of the most important but least understood parts of a capsule filling machine, especially in AF90T and ZRO90.
Many fill-weight, powder leakage, and dosing problems are actually related to the wiper block.
________________________________________
What is a Wiper Block?
The wiper block is a component that:
Levels Powder
Removes Excess Powder
Pushes Powder Into Dosing Area
before filling occurs.
Think of it as a mini bulldozer for powder.

     ↓
Wiper Block
